AppWorld relationship-check validation, 22 November 2026

This is a post-hoc extension of the older-archive pilot, not a new held-out study.
The frozen resolver and 30 task IDs were chosen before this validation run.
No 2026 runs enter the manuscript experiment.
The original cohort was selected after exploratory screens of both older and
newer archives because it contained usable saved-contact mistakes. The analysis
does not estimate their prevalence in current models.

Claim: for the supported role-based requests, an independently resolved
permission set can identify real wrong-recipient sends that personal-contact
membership cannot flag. The experiment does not transfer the WorkBench history
contrast or measure prevention.

Controls and checks:
* Reconstruct every selected task/configuration from original final SQL state,
  including executions without sends. Do not rely on saved pilot verdicts.
* Validate initial-state prefixes and the task-owner sender. Match every scored
  message to the earlier extraction independently, by immutable message ID.
* The resolver receives only instruction text and original contact data. It
  never receives ground-truth answers, actual chosen recipients or task IDs.
* Compute scoring sets separately from original benchmark records, including
  the independent company-manager relation for the drafted-purchase task.
* Score all committed sends in the selected tasks, including correct ones.
  Report no-send executions and unsupported task coverage separately.
* Count messages, executions and task families separately. Report a first-error
  per execution sensitivity analysis and exclude the largest repeated-send
  family as a second sensitivity check.
* Verify that an opaque receipt of the resolved set and committed destinations
  preserves the raw check's decision. This is post-hoc serialization, not a
  deployed receipt adapter or live intervention.

The addition is justified if real non-owner, saved-contact errors remain across
multiple tasks and executions, independent resolution agrees with the scoring
records, and the check does not falsely flag recipient-correct sends. Any
disagreement or false flag must be reported. The study remains retrospective,
schema-specific and dependent on accurate trusted contact relationships.
